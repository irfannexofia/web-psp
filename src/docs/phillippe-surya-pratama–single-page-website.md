## 🔧 Layanan Kami

Kami menawarkan dua layanan unggulan yang menjadi fondasi reputasi PSP: **Welding Service** dan **Valve Repair Service**.

### 1) Welding Service

**Expert Industrial Welding for Equipment Repair & Fabrication**
Penanganan **crack repair**, **reinforcement**, **build-up worn parts**, dan **custom fabrication**.

**Metode:** SMAW, GTAW (TIG), FCAW, GMAW (MIG), Hardfacing & Overlay.
**Material:** Carbon steel, stainless, alloy, cast iron, non-ferrous.
**Aplikasi:** Tank & vessel repair, structural reinforcement, custom metal fabrication.
**Industri:** Mining, Oil & Gas, Power, Cement, Steel, Petrochemical, Manufacturing.

**Nilai tambah (konten):** dokumentasi foto sebelum–sesudah, deskripsi singkat spesifikasi material & sambungan, serta highlight keselamatan kerja (PPE, area aman, inspeksi NDT bila dibutuhkan).

### 2) Valve Repair Service

**Comprehensive Valve Repair, Reconditioning & Testing**
Tahapan layanan: **inspection & diagnostic**, **disassembly & cleaning**, **component repair or replacement (seats, stems, discs, seals)**, **lapping & machining**, **reassembly & calibration**, **hydrostatic/pneumatic pressure testing**, diakhiri **final quality check & reporting**.

**Valve Types:** Gate, Globe, Ball, Check, Butterfly, Relief, Control.
**Standar:** API, ASME, ANSI, OEM.
**Industri:** Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Power Plant, Water Treatment, Pulp & Paper, Chemical, Food & Beverage.

**Nilai tambah (konten):** cantumkan contoh kasus (mis. kebocoran pada seat, erosi pada stem) dan dampak perbaikan (tightness meningkat, siklus hidup komponen bertambah, downtime berkurang).
